ABOUT MONTECELLO

A place to breathe.

Located in Portsmouth, Montecello Heights gives you a comfortable place to settle in while taking in views of the garden, bay and harbor. It's an ideal spot to enjoy a slower side of Dominica while still being close to places worth exploring.

After a day of sightseeing, hiking or discovering the coast, come back to a spacious home where you can cook, unwind and enjoy your own space.

FIND US

Your Dominican escape starts here.

Montecello Heights is located in Portsmouth, making it a convenient base for exploring the north of Dominica. Cabrits National Park, the Indian River and Prince Rupert Bay are nearby, with restaurants, shops and other essentials within easy reach. Whether you're here for nature, adventure or a slower island getaway, you'll have a good starting point for exploring the area.
